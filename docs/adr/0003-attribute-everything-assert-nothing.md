# Attribute everything, assert nothing

Nothing in Brief verifies a claim against the world, so a Report has no standing to call anything a fact. We decided that every sentence in a Report is tied to who said it, that "verified fact" is not a category, and that when creators disagree the Report preserves the Split by name rather than averaging it into a verdict. Attention (how many of the videos we processed touched a Development) is a fact about our own corpus, never about truth, and unusual coverage is measured against the Topic's own history, not against YouTube.

## Considered options

A roundup with verdicts ("X is now the standard") was rejected: many creators saying one thing in one week is one story spreading, and a verdict would launder that into a finding.
